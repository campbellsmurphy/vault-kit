# 02: Areas

**Ongoing responsibilities and standards you maintain over time.** No deadline.

Areas are the parts of your work and life that do not "finish". You maintain a
level of quality in them indefinitely.

## Examples

- `team-leadership/`: your team lead practices, one to one templates, retros
- `methodology/`: standards, guidance, how the firm expects work done
- `dev-environment/`: your tooling, prompts you reuse
- `claude-code/`: this setup itself, plus agent written reports

## Conventions

- One folder per area. The folder is long lived, the notes inside it evolve.
- An `index.md` per area summarises current state and links to the most used
  notes. Refreshed during `/lint-wiki`.
- If something here grows a deadline and a defined outcome, promote it to
  `01-projects/`.

## Reserved

- `claude-code/reports/` is where agent written reports go (lint, sweeps,
  triage). Never `daily/`.

## Directory

Add one line per area as you create them.
