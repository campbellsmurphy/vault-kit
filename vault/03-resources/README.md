# 03: Resources

**Reference material, organised by topic.** Not tied to a project, not a
personal responsibility, just things you want to consult later.

This is where the LLM wiki actually lives. Most pages here are agent maintained:
when you `/ingest-url` a paper, post or doc, the agent compiles its content into
one or more topic pages here, updating cross references along the way.

## Examples

- `topics/llms/`
- `topics/python/`
- `topics/audit-standards/` (public material only, no client information)
- `topics/people/` (public figures, authors, colleagues, no sensitive data)

## Conventions

- Filenames are `kebab-case-topic-name.md`.
- Each page opens with a short **TL;DR** and closes with a **Sources** section
  listing every URL or source file path it was distilled from.
- Wikilinks everywhere. The graph is the value.
- Anything that becomes a project gets referenced from `01-projects/`, not moved.
- Topic pages are the most reusable part of the vault and the most likely to be
  shared or quoted. Keep them free of client identifying detail entirely, even
  aliased: the general lesson belongs here, the engagement specific one belongs
  in the project folder.
