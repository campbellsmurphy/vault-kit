# 04: Archive

**Completed projects and retired areas.** Nothing here is being actively worked
on.

Move folders here when a project's outcome is achieved (or you have decided to
abandon it), or an area is no longer relevant.

## Why archive instead of delete

Future you will want to grep these. They are also good material for the agent:
past projects often contain the pattern you are about to repeat.

## Conventions

- Keep the folder name and prepend the year archived: `2026-learn-duckdb/`.
- Do not reorganise on the way in. Move as is.
- Archived material is still LLM readable and still subject to the same
  confidentiality rules. Archiving is not a retention decision. If something has
  a deletion obligation attached to it, delete it rather than archiving it.
